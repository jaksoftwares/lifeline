# LOGO DESIGN BRIEF — LIFELINE

Design a premium, professional, minimalist visual identity and complete logo system for **Lifeline**, a healthcare referral and emergency patient-transfer coordination platform.

### 1. Brand context

Lifeline connects referring healthcare facilities, receiving hospitals, ambulance teams and healthcare operations personnel through one coordinated patient-transfer workflow.

The platform helps coordinate:
- Patient referrals between healthcare facilities.
- Receiving-hospital communication and acceptance.
- Ambulance assignment and dispatch.
- Patient transfer tracking.
- Arrival confirmation and clinical handover.

Lifeline is a healthcare coordination and technology brand, not simply an ambulance-booking application or hospital management system.

**Brand tagline:** Connected care. Coordinated transfers.

**Brand personality:** Trustworthy, calm, intelligent, modern, dependable, human and professional.

### 2. Creative direction

Create a distinctive, sophisticated and minimalist logo that communicates:

- Connection between healthcare facilities.
- Continuity throughout the patient-transfer journey.
- Coordinated movement and communication.
- Trust, reliability and patient-centred care.
- Modern healthcare technology.

Explore a unique abstract symbol built around a connected pathway, linked points, a continuous route or a subtle protective form.

The symbol should be simple enough to recognise at a glance and distinctive enough to become an independent brand identifier.

A subtle medical reference may be incorporated, but avoid obvious or generic medical imagery.

### 3. Brand colours

Use the official proposed Lifeline palette:

- Lifeline Navy: #12304A
- Lifeline Teal: #0F8B8D
- Deep Teal: #086B70
- Soft Teal: #DDF3F1
- White: #FFFFFF

Navy should communicate trust and institutional credibility. Teal should introduce a sense of connection, progress and modernity.

The logo must also work perfectly in:
- Solid black.
- Solid white.
- Single-colour navy.
- Single-colour teal.

Do not depend on gradients, shadows or multiple colours to make the symbol recognisable.

### 4. Wordmark

The name must read:

**Lifeline**

Use a clean, contemporary sans-serif typeface. Explore typography inspired by Inter, Manrope or a similarly refined geometric/humanist sans-serif.

Typography should feel:
- Professional.
- Balanced.
- Modern.
- Highly legible.
- Confident without appearing aggressive.

Use carefully considered letter spacing, proportions and optical alignment.

Do not alter the spelling or introduce additional words into the primary wordmark.

### 5. Required logo variations

Create a complete, visually consistent logo family.

**A. Primary horizontal logo**
- Symbol positioned to the left.
- Lifeline wordmark positioned to the right.
- Primary navy and teal colour treatment.
- This is the main logo for the website, application header, presentations and official documents.

**B. Stacked logo**
- Symbol positioned above the wordmark.
- Balanced vertical composition.
- Suitable for posters, presentations, cover pages and square layouts.

**C. Symbol-only logo**
- A standalone version of the brand symbol.
- No text.
- Must remain identifiable at very small sizes.
- Suitable for mobile app icons, browser favicons, profile images and compact navigation.

**D. Monochrome logo**
- Complete logo in solid black or dark navy.
- No gradients or colour variations.
- Suitable for printing and restricted-colour applications.

**E. Reversed logo**
- White symbol and wordmark.
- Designed for navy or dark-coloured backgrounds.
- Maintain strong contrast and legibility.

**F. Compact logo**
- A simplified horizontal composition for narrow navigation bars and small interface spaces.
- Ensure the symbol and wordmark remain legible.

**G. Tagline lockup**
- Primary horizontal logo accompanied by:
  "Connected care. Coordinated transfers."
- Maintain a clear hierarchy between the brand name and tagline.
- Do not make the tagline part of the core symbol.

### 6. App icon and favicon

Create a dedicated square app-icon composition using the symbol only.

Requirements:
- Recognisable at 16 × 16, 32 × 32, 48 × 48 and 512 × 512 pixels.
- Strong silhouette.
- Simple geometry.
- Good contrast.
- Suitable for rounded-square mobile app icon masks.
- No small text.
- No unnecessary decorative elements.

Show the symbol on both white and navy backgrounds.

### 7. Visual quality requirements

The final identity should feel comparable in quality and restraint to established international healthcare technology and enterprise SaaS brands.

Aim for:
- Timeless rather than trendy.
- Minimal rather than decorative.
- Distinctive rather than generic.
- Professional rather than playful.
- Human rather than overly clinical.

Use precise geometry, balanced proportions, clean curves and consistent stroke or shape relationships.

### 8. Strict exclusions

Do not use:
- Generic medical crosses as the main logo.
- Heartbeat or ECG lines as the central design.
- Ambulance silhouettes.
- Stethoscopes.
- Human figures holding hands.
- Generic location pins.
- Overly complex network diagrams.
- Excessive gradients.
- 3D effects.
- Drop shadows.
- Clip-art aesthetics.
- Crowded or overly decorative compositions.
- Generic stock-logo concepts.

Do not imitate an existing healthcare company's logo or use another organisation's trademarked symbol.

### 9. Presentation and deliverables

Present the proposed identity as a polished professional brand identity board.

Include:

1. Primary horizontal logo.
2. Stacked logo.
3. Symbol-only mark.
4. Monochrome version.
5. Reversed white version on navy.
6. Compact logo.
7. Tagline lockup.
8. Mobile application icon.
9. Browser favicon preview.
10. Colour palette with HEX values.
11. Typography recommendation.
12. Logo-on-light and logo-on-dark applications.

Show the variations as a coherent family. Every version must use the same underlying symbol and wordmark design. Do not create unrelated symbols for different applications.

Use a clean white presentation background, generous spacing, restrained typography and a premium brand-guideline presentation style.

### 10. Final creative objective

Create a logo that makes Lifeline look like a credible, established healthcare technology company capable of serving hospitals, ambulance networks, county health systems and national healthcare institutions.

The identity should communicate one central idea:

**Lifeline connects the healthcare system around the patient's journey.**

The final result must be distinctive, memorable, scalable, technically reproducible and suitable for long-term use across digital products, mobile applications, institutional partnerships, official documents and physical branding.
